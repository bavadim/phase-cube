# Music and audio

The full industrial-rave arrangement “Цех горного короля” is project material.
The A theme adapts Edvard Grieg, In the Hall of the Mountain King,
Mutopia edition 1888 (Public Domain):
https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=1888
No commercial recording or Frank Klepacki melody is sampled.

Selected percussion and TX81Z Piano 1 samples come from VCSL,
Versilian Community Sample Library, contributors / Versilian Studios,
CC0-1.0, revision c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e:
https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

song-assets.js embeds the derived 13-part backing and eight riff fragments.
audio-manifest.json records source hashes and the adaptation.
